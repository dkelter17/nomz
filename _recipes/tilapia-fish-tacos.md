### Ingredients

Cabbage Slaw:

- [ ] 2 cups sliced cabbage
- [ ] ¼ cup mayo
- [ ] 2 Tbsp chipotle in adobo, minced
- [ ] 2 tsp lime juice
- [ ] 2 tsp honey
- [ ] 2 scallions, sliced
- [ ] Salt & pepper

Fish:

- [ ] 2 fillets tilapia
- [ ] 1 cup panko
- [ ] 1 cup flour
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] Tortillas
- [ ] Avocados (optional)

### Instructions:

1. Combine ingredients in cabbage ingredient list and set aside.
2. Bread tilapia in flour, egg, panko, then fry.
3. Spoon some tilapia and cabbage slaw into tortilla. Serve.
